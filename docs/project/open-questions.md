# Open Questions

Use this file for unresolved questions that materially affect the project.

Remove questions once they are answered and move durable answers into the
appropriate project documentation.

## Business

- What does the company do?
- What is the primary website objective?

## Customer

- Who is the primary audience?
- What problem are they trying to solve?

## Offering

- What products or services are currently offered?
- Which offering is most important?

## Conversion

- What should the visitor do next?
- Is the primary action an enquiry, booking, purchase, call, WhatsApp message, or something else?

## Brand

- Is there an existing visual identity?
- What must be retained?
- What should the brand feel like?

## Assets

- What photography, video, logos, testimonials, reviews, and other assets exist?

## Technical

- What functionality is actually required?
- Are there any integrations or third-party systems?
